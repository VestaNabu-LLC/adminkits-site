One check, in full

D1, one of the 10 checks the workbook says to do first. The other 59 follow the same pattern.

D1

Does any permission scheme let the public browse a space?

High if found
Why it matters
Browse spaces granted to Public lets anyone on the internet view the space and its work items, without Jira access. It's how a space is made public on purpose, and the worst case when it's an accident.
Where to look
Jira settings (the cog in Jira's top bar) → Work items → Permission Schemes (under Work item attributes) → each scheme → Browse spaces.
Good looks like
No Public on Browse spaces, except for spaces you mean to publish.
If not
Remove Public from Browse spaces in every scheme a private space uses.

Free sites can't make spaces public.

Atlassian sources: Use permissions to make a space publicly visible; Space access and configuration permissions
